Beluga Deep: Ocean Explorer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · App: Beluga Deep: Ocean Explorer (Android, package com.whitesun.belugadeep; iPhone, bundle ID com.whitesun.belugadeep) · Developer: WhiteSun (Sefa Akgüneş)

Summary

Beluga Deep does not ask for an account, does not collect personal data and does not share or sell any data. The game works fully offline and your progress stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your pearls, upgrades, discovered species, rescued friends, found treasures, discovered air pockets, explored map, deepest dive, daily golden pearl streak, play statistics and your settings (sound, music, vibration, effects, language) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the app. It may be included in your device's own backup if you have backup enabled; that backup is handled by Google or Apple according to your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions

The app only uses the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings. It has no internet access and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. The sky in the game follows your device's clock (day, sunset, night); the time is only read on your device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Ads and third parties

This version contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
