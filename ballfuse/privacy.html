Ballfuse: Brick Breaker Rogue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · App: Ballfuse: Brick Breaker Rogue (Android, package com.whitesun.ballfuse; iPhone, bundle ID com.whitesun.ballfuse) · Developer: WhiteSun (Sefa Akgüneş)

Summary

Ballfuse does not ask for an account, does not collect personal data and does not share or sell any data. The game works fully offline and your progress stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your best wave, gems, permanent upgrades, discovered balls, daily streak, an unfinished run and your settings (sound, music, vibration, effects, language) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the app. It may be included in your device's own Android backup if you have backup enabled; that backup is handled by Google according to your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions

The app only uses the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings. It has no internet access and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Ads and third parties

This version contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

iPhone (App Store) version

The iPhone version of Ballfuse currently shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not use the internet, does not access the advertising identifier and does not track you. Your game data is stored only on your iPhone (and may be included in your own iCloud or device backup); uninstalling the app deletes it. The advertising section above applies to the Android version only. If ads are added to the iPhone version later, this policy will be updated first.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
