Ballfuse: Brick Breaker Rogue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · App: Ballfuse: Brick Breaker Rogue (Android, package com.whitesun.ballfuse; iPhone, bundle ID com.whitesun.ballfuse) · Developer: WhiteSun (Sefa Akgüneş)

Summary

Ballfuse does not ask for an account and does not sell your data. Your game progress stays on your device. Starting with version 1.1 the Android version shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses some device data to serve and measure ads, and offers an optional one-time "Premium" purchase through Google Play that removes ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your best wave, gems, permanent upgrades, discovered balls, daily streak, an unfinished run, whether you own Premium and your settings (sound, music, vibration, effects, language) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the app. It may be included in your device's own Android backup if you have backup enabled; that backup is handled by Google according to your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions

The Android app uses internet access (to load ads and to check Premium purchases with Google Play), the advertising ID permission (used by Google AdMob), the Google Play billing permission (for the optional Premium purchase) and the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

From version 1.1 the Android game shows full-screen ads between runs (never during a run, and not in your first runs) and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to revive, reroll cards or double your gems. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, personalize (where allowed) and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time with "Privacy options" in the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The game contains no analytics or tracking SDKs other than Google AdMob.

Premium purchase (Google Play)

Premium is an optional one-time purchase that removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see or store your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium so it can turn ads off (and restore Premium after a reinstall). See policies.google.com/privacy.

iPhone (App Store) version

The iPhone version of Ballfuse currently shows no ads, offers no purchases and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not use the internet, does not access the advertising identifier and does not track you. Your game data is stored only on your iPhone (and may be included in your own iCloud or device backup); uninstalling the app deletes it. The advertising and Premium sections above apply to the Android version only. If ads are added to the iPhone version later, this policy will be updated first.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
